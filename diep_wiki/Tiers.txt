= Tiers =

{{Merge|Upgrades|Tiers are a construction based off of the upgrade system and isn't universal outside of tanks. Info from this page will most likely overwrite info on the Upgrades page.}}

[[File:Final_Tier1.png|thumb|300px|The official class tree as of February 12th, 2025. The class tree can be opened in game by pressing Y on the keyboard in Diep.io.]]

{{PA}} are separate [[tanks|Tank Classes]], organized with the characteristics of their weapon(s).

Tiers are represented in the Class Tree, shown on the picture to the right, as a series of concentric rings containing every tank in the game. It can be displayed in-game by [[Controls|holding the Y key]]. In the direct center of the Class Tree is the player's tank, which is Tier 1. The first ring contains Tier 2 (Level 15) tanks, the second ring contains Tier 3 (Level 30) tanks, and the outermost ring contains Tier 4 (Level 45) tanks. Areas shaded black on the tree indicate an unavailable [[Upgrades|Upgrade]] in that tier; for example, the player must press ignore once after the [[Basic Tank]] to get to the [[Smasher]].

There is a recurring pattern of colors in the upgrade selection menu and the Class Tree. The first tank in an upgrade branch will always have a <span style="color: #8efffb; font-weight: bold;">light blue</span> background. The second tank will be <span style="color: #b3ff8e; font-weight: bold;">green</span>. The third tank will be <span style="color: #ff8e8e; font-weight: bold;">red</span>. The fourth tank will be <span style="color: #ffeb8e; font-weight: bold;">yellow</span>. The fifth will be <span style="color: #8eb2ff; font-weight: bold;">dark blue</span>. This is only used for [[Smasher]], [[Battleship]], [[Auto Trapper]], and [[Glider]]. The sixth option will be <span style="color: #b58eff; font-weight: bold;">purple</span>. The [[Factory]], [[Auto Tank (tank)|Auto Tank]] and [[Firework]] are currently the only tanks with this color. The color order can also be found in the tank’s [[Stats|Stat]] buttons, which are color-reversed compared to the pattern in the class tree. The game mode selection buttons and colored [[Achievements]] list also use this pattern. Regardless of speculation, these colors do not seem to represent anything other than a simple order pattern to more easily distinguish tanks apart when quickly selecting an upgrade for now.

== Tier 2: Level 15 ==
Upon reaching <b>Level 15</b>, the player may choose to upgrade to one of these <b>Tier 2</b> specializations, four in total, or ignore them to get to the [[Smasher]] at level 30 or the [[Auto Tank (tank)|Auto Tank]] at level 45.

=== Tank Upgrades ===
{| style="margin: auto; text-align: center;"
|[[File:twin icon 3.png|100px|link=Twin]]
|<b>[[Twin]]</b><br />Choosing the Twin class grants an extra frontal Cannon while bullet power is slightly reduced. The high rate of fire increases the recoil.
|-
|[[File:sniper icon 4.png|100px|link=Sniper]]
|<b>[[Sniper]]</b><br />Choosing the Sniper Class grants a broader field of view allowing the player to see further. Bullet Speed is also increased, increasing the bullet's distance. However, Reload speed decreases noticeably. Damage is not increased.
|-
|[[File:machine gun icon 4.png|100px|link=Machine Gun]]
|<b>[[Machine Gun]]</b><br />The Machine Gun sprays its Bullets from a trapezoid on the front. It fires double the rate of a basic tank, the same rate of the Twin. It is less accurate but it covers a wider area. Bullet damage is slightly higher than the twin but less than the basic.
|-
|[[File:flank guard icon 3.png|100px|link=Flank Guard]]
|<b>[[Flank Guard]]</b><br />Choosing the Flank Guard class adds a Cannon on the rear. This allows the player to shoot in both directions to defend their back. Because both Cannons have identical Recoil, the Flank Guard effectively has no recoil.
|}

== Tier 4: Level 45 ==
Upon reaching <b>Level 45</b>, the player may select one of these <b>Tier 4</b> specializations. This is the final tier that most tanks can reach and is also the final level that any player can reach unless they press H in [[Domination]] game mode to become the [[Dominator]], which is fixed at Level 75 or press H in [[Mothership (Game Mode)|Mothership]] game mode to become the [[Mothership (Tank)|Mothership]], which is fixed at Level 140. There are thirty-three in total, of which five have 2 different upgrade paths and two have 3 paths, making a total of forty-two tanks appear in the outermost ring of the Class Tree.

It is recommended for a level 30 Twin to avoid upgrading to Triple Shot until getting to level 45, as a Triple Shot's Bullets are very spread out and have little direct firepower. The triplets' bullets are actually slightly weaker than a twins' bullets to balance out the tanks' high firepower.

== Trivia == 
*All Tier 4 tanks are 126-175 pixels wide.
*The basic tank has a surprisingly strong bullet compared to other bullet shooting tank classes, only weaker than Trapper branch traps, Overseer branch drones, and Destroyer branch bullets.
*If there were to be a seventh tank from a branch, the background color would be <span style="color: #fb8eff; font-weight: bold;">pink</span>, as shown by the Game Mode selection, the colored [[Achievements]] list and the reversed Stats colors. For an eighth, it would be <span style="color: #fdcead; font-weight: bold;">tan</span>. It is unknown what a ninth tank would be, but since the colors used by the achievements list cycle through the eight colors, it would most likely be <span style="color: #8efffb; font-weight: bold;">light blue</span>.
*There was a running theory that the background color of the Upgrade icon corresponds to the base Stat debuff it gets, as it typically matches the color of the respective Stat bar. 
**There are several issues with this:
***If the background color were to depend on the tank’s Stat debuff, there wouldn’t be color variants, as seen in the [[Overtrapper]], [[Auto Gunner]], [[Streamliner]], and [[Battleship]], for example.
***The Stat de-buffs aren’t necessarily true: For instance, the Battleship may have a dark blue background, but with its notoriously fast Drones, it certainly doesn’t get a de-buff to the Bullet/Drone Speed.
****In general, the theory can be debunked with simple Sandbox testing.
*Most of the fully removed tanks had a light blue upgrade background. The only removed tank with a different background color was the Auto 4.
*These Stats are not changed upon upgrading to every tank (which means no buff or debuff): Health Regeneration, Movement Speed, Max Health.
*The [[Auto 3]] and [[Gunner]] are the only Tier 3 tanks to have no Tier 4 upgrades exclusive to them. Strangely, both share an upgrade between them, the [[Auto Gunner]].
*The [[Overseer]] and [[Destroyer]] have the most Tier 4 upgrades of all Tier 3 tanks, with 6 options. It is followed by the [[Trapper]] with 5 upgrades. All other Tier 3 tanks have only 2 or 3 Tier 4 upgrades.
*The [[Battleship]] is the only Tier 4 tank capable of being upgraded to from 3 of the 4 Tier 1 tanks.
*The [[Smasher]], [[Sprayer]], and [[Auto Tank (tank)|Auto Tank]] are currently the only three tanks that jump Tiers when being upgraded to; the Smasher and Sprayer can only be upgraded to by a tank 2 tiers lower than the upgrade's tier, and require the player to reach 30 levels past the previous upgrade's threshold, instead of 15. The Auto Tank upgrades straight from the basic tank and is the only tank to jump two Tiers.
*There are fifty-four normal tanks in total: one Tier 1 tank, four Tier 2 tanks, thirteen Tier 3 tanks, and thirty-six Tier 4 tanks.<br />
