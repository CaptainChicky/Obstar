The {{PA}} is the fifth strongest [[Polygons|Polygon]] in [[Diep.io]]. It gives 1,000 [[Levels|XP]] upon destruction and is noticeably stronger than a regular [[Square]]. The only Polygons known to be stronger are the [[Pentagon]], [[Green Triangle]], the [[Green Pentagon]], and the [[Alpha Pentagon]]. It has equal health but less body damage compared to a [[Pentagon]]. These have a one in a million chance of spawning instead of a normal square.

== Trivia ==
[[File:DiepGreenSquare.png|thumb|63px|A clear image of a green square.]]
*A Level 1 [[Tank]] will need fifteen hits to kill a Green Square. This results in a reward of 66.67 EXP per hit.
**If you manage to destroy a Green Square at Level 1, you will skip the next 15 Levels, instantly landing you at Level 16.
*A [[Necromancer]] can make this Polygon one of its [[Drones]] by crashing into it or touching it with its current drones causing it to break and becoming one of the Drones with the same strength of a regular Necromancer Drone. However, the green square drone will give you 1000 EXP when it is killed, unlike other drones which only give you 10 EXP.
*When you are killed by this shape, it will say “You were killed by: Square” instead of “You were killed by: Green Square”, as they are practically the same thing, only the Green Square is much rarer.
*The [[Pentagon]] has the same HP as the Green Square, which is 100 HP.
**Although they have the same health, the Green Square is easier to destroy, as their base body damage stat is different, with the Green Square having 8 body damage with (same as Square) and Pentagon having 12 body damage.
*It can be found in most likely [[2 Teams]], [[Domination]], [[4 Teams]], and any other game mode. It is much rarer in [[Sandbox]] mode because the map is smaller and it won't have much room to spawn in.
