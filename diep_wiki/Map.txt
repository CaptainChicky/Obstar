= Map =

The {{PA}}, also called the <b>Minimap</b>, appears in the lower right corner of the screen and displays an overview of the layout of the player’s current [[Game Modes|Game Mode]], along with their location & direction facing (indicated with a black triangular pointer). All Game Modes have a map that shows the outline of the area, the [[Team Deathmatch|Team Bases]], [[Dominator]]s (only in [[Domination]]) and your tank’s location. It does not show any other Players or Bosses though.

== Game Modes ==
All of the Game Modes have different map layouts. [[FFA]], [[Tag]] & [[Sandbox]] have a very similar map but don’t have any [[Team Deathmatch|Team Bases]] as there is no need for Teams.

=== 2 Teams / 4 Teams ===
[[4 Teams]] has four Teams consisting of blue, purple, green and red, and [[2 Teams]] Game Mode consists of two Teams, one red (right) and the other blue (left), which are placed at opposite sides of the map spreading from the top to the bottom.

=== Domination ===
[[Domination]] consists of two [[Team Deathmatch|Team Bases]] that can be randomly placed in any of the four corners of the map, though the Blue Base will always be on the left-hand side and vice versa for the Red Base. Each Base is the size of the 4 Teams Game Mode Bases (and also contain [[Base Drones]]), but also add four much smaller Bases in the middle which are used by the [[Dominator]]s which the player can defeat and then [[Controls|press H]] on their keyboard to control. The game objective and win condition are for the player’s team to defeat all four Dominators and take over all four Bases.

=== Maze ===
[[Maze]] consists of a randomly generated map terrain of walls that no tank body or bullet can enter, although [[Barrels]] and other Weapons can enter Maze Walls.

=== Tag ===
[[Tag]] mode used to have a [[Dominator]] that was in the middle of the Map, where the [[Pentagon Nest]] is. Now [[Tag]] has a map that is similar to [[FFA]], but the map shrinks over time. This was probably to make catching tanks easier if only one tank is still rebelling the winning Team.

== History ==
*The Map was added on May 31st, 2016.
