The {{PA}} is the third rarest confirmed [[Polygons|Polygon]] in [[Diep.io]], and the third strongest in regard to health; the strongest being the Green [[Alpha Pentagon]]. The Green Pentagon grants a total of 13,000 EXP when destroyed. There have been very few confirmed sightings of it, but it allegedly has ten times the health of a regular [[Pentagon]]. These have a one in a million chance of spawning instead of a normal Pentagon.

== Trivia ==
*It is the third most valuable Polygon in the game, awarding 13,000 worth to your score when destroyed. A player killing it at Level 1 will directly rise to Level 37. In [[Domination]], [[Tag]], or [[Survival]], killing this Polygon can allow the player to rise to level 45 almost immediately, due to the 2x score and 3x score, respectively, [[Levels|Experience]] gain bonus in those [[Game Modes]].
*When you are killed by this shape, it will say “You were killed by: Pentagon” instead of “You were killed by: Green Pentagon”.
*It has a third of the health of an [[Alpha Pentagon]], making it the third most durable polygon in the game.
*Like other green Polygons, the Green Pentagon's color code is 0x8AFF69.
*Unlike Alpha Pentagons, it doesn't just spawn in the [[Pentagon Nest]] (although it is more likely to, as more Pentagons spawn there).
**Even though it is more common for these to spawn in the Pentagon Nest, it still isn't very common.
