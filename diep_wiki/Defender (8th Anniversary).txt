This variant of [[Defender]] was added on 16th April 2024, [[Diep.io]]'s 8th anniversary.

== Design ==
This variant Defender features a large Red [[Triangle]] as its body with three evenly-spaced trap [[Launchers]], one on each side. Three [[Auto Turrets]] are placed on top of it on each corner. It also has three evenly-spaced Spawners, one on each side, each of them overlapping a trap Launcher. The Spawners spawn drones, each of them is a yellow triangle with three evenly-spaced and smaller trap [[Launchers]], one on each side.

== Technical ==

=== Stats ===
Like every other boss, the Defender has 3,000 HP, with a slow heal rate. Its launchers have a high reload, and its Auto Turrets have an average fire rate. Its traps are normal-sized with high penetration and damage. Auto Turrets and bullets act identically to the [[Auto 5]] but have a lower speed. The Defender also moves very slowly. The traps spawned by the drones are slightly smaller than the regular traps. This Defender has up to 6 drones.

=== Miscellaneous ===

* Upon destruction, it and its drones will disappear, it awards 30,000 experience, giving it to the player who deals the final shot.
* When the Defender happens to drift near a wall, it will immediately change its path to move parallel to the wall.
* It is not targeted by the Base Protectors unless it provokes them and the Base itself has an effect on it.
* The Defender has auto-spin passively enabled.
* Its minions circle around the main tank like how drones AI behave on an Overseer when idle. 
* Similarly to [[Arena Closer|Arena Closers]], it is not slowed down at all when bumping into [[Polygons]].
* The Defender suffers from its own Auto Turrets’ [[Stats|Recoil]].
* When idle, the Defender slowly circles the arena until it sees a player.

== Strategy ==

* '''Strong Against:''' Low DPS tanks, Melee classes, [[Drone]] classes, such as the Overlord and Overseer. (Unless distracted), Spread classes, most [[Trapper|Trappers]].
* '''Weak Against:''' High DPS, [[Assassin]] branch, [[Predator|Predators]], [[Streamliner|Streamliners]] and other focused RoF classes, [[Destroyer]] branch, [[Overlord|Overlords]] and [[Factory|Factories]] if caught off guard.

=== Against the Defender ===

* All [[Defender#Against the Defender|strategies against the regular Defender]] apply, with a slightly higher difficulty due to the extra layer of protection provided by its drones and their traps.

== Trivia ==

* Like other 8th Anniversary Variant of bosses, its name shown above the tank constantly shifted between every second in a cycle of blue, red, purple, green, and then back to blue (based off the [[4 Teams|Teams Colors]]).
* If you are playing as it, you can control the Mounted Turrets.
* It is the first entity to use 3 types of ammunition, using Traps, Bullets and Minions.
