The <b>Region selector</b> is a menu in [[Diep.io]] that allows the player to choose the location of the servers that they can join. There are currently five, which are Frankfurt, Atlanta, Sydney, São Paulo and Singapore.

Just like [[Game Modes]], Players can switch regions before they enter a room by clicking their respective buttons.

== History ==
* The Region Selector was added on 25th February 2022.
* Before 4th January 2024, the five regions were San Francisco, Frankfurt, Atlanta, Sydney, and Tokyo.
* São Paulo was added on 28th December 2025.

== Trivia ==
* It has been theorized<sup>[by who?]</sup> that the Region Selector was added in order to get rid of the several Server Selector scripts made by the players and to avoid the hassle of using a VPN.
