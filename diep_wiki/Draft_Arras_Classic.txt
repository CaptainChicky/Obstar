== Information in Changelog ==

* Added a new event called Classic!
* In this event, you do not have the luxury of instant level 45! You will need to farm or fight for your place among the top.
* There is a new shape mutation within this event's ecosystem, and it's a rather vicious one. Proceed with caution...

== Trivia ==

* In this gamemode you can't press '''N''' or play with enabled '''Auto-Level Up'''
* There was spawning [[Arras:Bosses|Arras.io Bosses]], but not spawning [[Bosses|Diep.io Bosses]]
* Classic like an [[Arras:Worlds Collide|Worlds Collide]]
* This gamemode released in 05.02.2024
* There was no portal spawning
* There is a new type of [https://diepio.fandom.com/wiki/Arras:Mutation#Crasher Crasher] called Polygon Crasher it also has beta and alpha variations as well
