The {{PA}} is an extremely rare [[Polygons|Polygon]] in [[Diep.io]], and the third strongest in regards to health. When killed, it rewards 2,500 [[Levels|XP]] to the player who deals the final shot. It is approximately 10 times stronger than a regular [[Triangle]]. The only two [[Polygons]] to be confirmed stronger than this one are the [[Green Pentagon]] and the [[Alpha Pentagon]]. These have a one in a million chance of spawning instead of a normal triangle.

== Trivia ==
*When the player is killed by this shape, the death screen will say “You were killed by: Triangle” instead of “You were killed by: Green Triangle”, much like every other Green Polygon in the game.
*The Green Triangle is very similar to the regular [[Triangle]] if the player uses the [[Console]] on triangles.
*The Green Triangle is the same size and shape as a regular triangle.
*It is the 2nd most rare [[Polygons|Polygon]], the rarest being the [[Green Pentagon]].
