The {{PA}} is the second strongest polygon in the Birthday mode, second only to the Alpha Pentagon. It is a yellow decagon with 5 auto turrets mounted at 72 degree angles. These auto turrets fire small bullets at nearby tanks with very high damage and bullet penetration.

== Strategy ==
*The Decagon deals extremely high [[Damage]] for how small the bullets are, easily able to kill low level tanks in a single volley.
*Most tanks will be unable to deal consistent damage due to the Decagon's high [[Bullet Penetration]], requiring them to shoot around the bullets.
*The easiest way to kill a Decagon is using a [[Destroyer]] class, as their bullets have enough penetration to beat the auto turret bullets and the large bullets deal large amounts of damage to the Decagon
*The auto-turrets will attempt to target where the player is going rather than they are. This can be abused to guarantee hits against the Decagon.
**Movement Speed is recommended, but not required as most of the time the auto turrets will heavily overshoot your position when moving left to right.
*[[Drone]] classes can maneuever their drones around the Decagon's bullets or out from the Decagon's auto-targeting range.
*[[Sniper]] classes can often out-range the Decagon to guarantee damage, although this will often take a very long time.
