= Spawners =

{{PA}} are a type of weapon that spawn [[Drones]].
== Appearance ==
<b>Spawners</b> look like gray trapezoids and appear on certain [[Tanks]] and [[Bosses]]. Some may appear narrower or tapered such as the case for [[Mothership]] and [[Battleship]] respectively. Spawners can spawn different kinds of drones depending on the [[Tiers|Tank]] (or represent the function of a class like [[Necromancer]] which cannot create drones directly).

== Wielders ==
*[[Overseer]]: 2 Drone Spawners.
*[[Overlord]]: 4 Drone Spawners.
*[[Necromancer]]: 2 Drone Spawners (non-functional).
*[[Manager]]: 1 Drone Spawner.
*[[Overtrapper]]: 1 Passive Drone Spawner and 1 Drone Spawner.
*[[Battleship]]: 4 Small Drone Spawners.
*[[Factory]]: 1 Factory Drone Spawner.
*[[Hybrid]]: 1 Secondary Drone Spawner.
*[[Mothership (Tank)|Mothership]]: 16 Drone Spawners.
*[[Guardian]]: 1 Crasher Spawner.
*[[Summoner]]: 4 Summoner Spawners.
*[[Fallen Overlord]]: 4 Drone Spawners.

==Trivia==
* The [[Battleship]] is the second (and to date only) class to use a small [[Drones|Drone]] spawner.
