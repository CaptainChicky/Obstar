The {{PA}} is the second rarest confirmed [[Polygons|Polygon]] in [[Diep.io]], and the second strongest in regard to health; the strongest being the Green [[Alpha Pentagon]]. The Green Hexagon grants a total of 150,000 EXP when destroyed. There have been very few confirmed sightings of it, but it allegedly has ten times the health of a regular [[Hexagon]]. These have a one in a million chance of spawning instead of a normal Hexagon.

== Trivia ==

* It is the second most valuable Polygon in the game, awarding 150,000 worth to your score when destroyed. A player killing it at Level 1 will directly rise to Level 45, no matter what mode they're in.
* When you are killed by this shape, it will say “You were killed by: Hexagon” instead of “You were killed by: Green Hexagon”.
* It has five times of the health of an [[Alpha Pentagon]], making it the second most durable polygon in the game.
* Like other green Polygons, the Green Hexagon's color code is 0x8AFF69.
* Due to how uncommon regular Hexagon is, its green variant is even rarer than Green Pentagon
