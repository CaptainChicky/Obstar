= Notifications =

{{PA}} are messages that show up in the middle-top of the screen when something special happens in the game. There are three types of notifications: those which are shown to the whole server as an announcement, those which are visible for a certain team and those which are only shown to a single player.

== Trivia ==
*There are currently more than 70 possible notifications in the game.
**There were formerly 84 notifications in total, but 32 of them were removed due to the removed content/features, as well as some new ones being added due to new content/features.
*The notification "The game crashed, refreshing page to recover from error" was found buried inside the game's source code before it was seen in-game.<ref name="JSCite1">{{PL|http://diep.io/c.js|Evidence}}</ref>
*If you kill a tank, then they are immediately killed, then they respawn immediately, the "you’ve killed" notification is present on your next round for a few seconds.
* Different notifications have different colored backgrounds.
** The "Arena Closed" and "Full team" messages are colored deep red.
** Mode notifications are colored deep blue (except for Sandbox's God mode).
** Any team-related messages are colored in the team's color. (The neutral color, which is shown when Dominators are being contested, is yellow.)
** All other messages are grey.
** For the 8th anniversary event, a new pink notification was added. The bosses spawn notifications were also changed to pink.
