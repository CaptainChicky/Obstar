The {{PA}} (formerly ''Firecracker'') is a Tier 4 tank in Diep.io. it upgrades from the [[Destroyer]] at level 45.
== Design ==
The Firework features a short Trapezoid barrel with a trapezoid base overlapping it (like Ranger or Dominator). The Trapezoid barrel also overlaps a thinner, longer Trapezoid

== Technical ==
When shooting, it shoots a hexagon which on right click explodes into 24 fragments arranged into 4 rings of 6. The fragments on each ring are arranged in the shape of a Hexagon, with alternating orientations. The firework itself also features a small cannon at the back that shoots small bullets to propel it forward with a base rate twice as fast as Tank's reload speed. The lifespan of the small bullets is short.

== Trivia ==

* During the beta testing phase, the tank was known as "Firecracker."
** The tank was originally planned to be upgraded from [[Shotgun]].
** The first iteration has the appearance similar to the [[Glider]] tank but shoots a 4-point-star shape bullet that explodes into a wave of small bullets. 
*** The tank would go through many more iterations before becoming the Firework of today. 
* This was the last tank to be added to Diep.io in 2025.
