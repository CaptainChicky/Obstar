The {{PA}} is a Tier 4 tank in Diep.io. It upgrades from the [[Tank]] at level 45.

== Design ==

Auto Tank has a Circular body with a cannon in front of the tank. with an addition of an auto turret on top like the [[Auto Gunner]], [[Auto Trapper]] and [[Auto Smasher]].
== Technical ==

It works like Basic, except that it has a top-mounted Auto turret on it.

Stats (Auto Turret):

* Bullet Speed: 1.2x Tank
* Bullet Penetration: 2 (+1.5 for each point in Bullet Penetration)
* Bullet Damage: 2.1 (+0.9 for each point in Bullet Damage)

== Strategy ==
* <b>Strong Against:</b>  Triplets (if the Auto Tank is skilled enough), Destroyers like [[Hybrid]] or [[Annihilator]].
* <b>Weak Against:</b> Sniper tanks like [[Ranger]] or [[Predator]].

=== As the Auto Tank ===

Play it like the Tank with an auto turret of course or play as you normally would with a [[Sprayer]], but with a bit less reload.
=== Against the Auto Tank ===
Use Sniper tanks like Ranger or Predator as the Auto Tank's Auto turret can't detect you from far away.

== Trivia ==

* It is the only Tier 4 tank that upgrades directly from the Tank.
* It is the second tank to have purple background. the other being the Factory.
* This is the 50th tank in the game, not counting deleted/merged tanks.
* It is the 4th tank to use an uncontrollable auto turret as its secondary weapon, the other 3 is the [[Auto Gunner]], [[Auto Trapper]] and [[Auto Smasher]].
