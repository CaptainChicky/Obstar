The original <b>Predator</b> was a Class that existed from 11th July 2016 to 9th August 2016. It was a [[Tiers|Tier 4]] tank that upgraded from [[Hunter]]. It shot bullets the same way Hunter did while having the ability to move the camera away from the tank by using [[Controls#Aim|Right Click or Shift]]. This tank was removed due to its scoping functionality being added to the [[Predator|X Hunter]], which also repurposed its name.

== Design ==
The Predator inherited [[Hunter]]'s two [[Cannons]] and added a trapezoid that's similar to [[Ranger]]'s own trapezoid. Predator's wider cannon is slightly thinner to accommodate for this trapezoid.

== History ==
*This tank was added on 11th July 2016 as a counterpart to the [[Predator|X Hunter]].
*It was removed on 9th August 2016.

== Trivia ==
*Predator's ID (30) goes completely unused in modern Diep due to it being removed.
*Predator's wider barrel is slightly thinner than Hunter's. This is so that the trapezoid would fit inside of the body.
**Hunter's barrel width is 56.7% of the tank's body, while Predator's is 54.6% of the tank's body.
**Humorously, it still slightly clips outside of the body.
*Predator and Mega Smasher can be found inside the legacy mobile version's asset from version 1.0.4 (1.2.0 for Mega Smasher) to 1.3.0. However, only Predator got added to the actual game.
**Like the Desktop version, Predator was eventually removed. Its ability was transferred to the X Hunter. However, X Hunter's name remain unchanged.
