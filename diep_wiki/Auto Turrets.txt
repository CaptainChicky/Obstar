<b>Mounted Turrets</b>, also known as <b>Auto Turrets</b> or simply as <b>Turrets</b>, are weapons that the [[Auto 3]], "Auto" tank upgrades, and [[Defender]] use. Mounted Turrets are gray circles with [[Cannons]] on them, attached to or on top of a tank that fire at nearby targets automatically without needing player input. When attached to the side of a tank (as seen on the Auto 3 and the Auto 5) only half of the circle beneath the Cannon can be seen, because of the tank's body covering some of the turret.

== Technical==
===Properties===
*Mounted Turrets are half the size of the tank body.
*Mounted Turrets share the same fire rate as standard Tank cannons.
* Mounted Turrets fire [[Bullets]] which are smaller (70%) than that of standard size.
* Mounted Turrets have slight recoil, like normal cannons.
*They have decent Bullet Penetration for their size.

===Targeting ===
*Mounted Turrets on the side of the tank can only turn their cannons a certain total angle.
** These '''can''' be controlled.
**Should an object leave their angle range, the next turret may take over.
*A Mounted Turret on the body of the tank can turn full circle. 
**These '''cannot''' be controlled (except Auto Smasher).

===When AI-controlled===
*The Mounted Turrets will shoot targets within range.
** The Mounted Turrets will not attack projectiles.
**The Mounted Turrets prioritize [[Tiers|Tanks]] over [[Polygons]].
**The Mounted Turrets will prioritize its attacker when the user takes damage.
*In [[Game Modes]] with teams, Mounted Turrets will not target players on your team, they will only target players on the enemy teams.

=== When it is possible to take control of them as the player===
* Players could control the Mounted Turrets(s) closest to their mouse by left-clicking, pressing space or having [[Auto Fire]] turned on.
*Right-clicking or pressing Shift would instead fire from the Mounted Turret most far away from the cursor.
** Mounted Turrets closest to their mouse would fire in the opposite direction, giving a boost.
*Multiple Turrets could be controlled if both were close enough to the mouse (only applied to Auto 3 and Auto 5).
*Mounted Turrets that are located in the middle of a tank (not including the Auto 3 or Auto 5) would slowly revolve around the tank when unable to be forced to target any entity.

==Users==

=== Tanks ===
*[[Auto 3]]: Has three evenly spaced controllable Mounted Turrets attached to the tank.
*[[Auto 5]]: Has five (previously [[Auto 5#History|four]]) evenly spaced controllable Mounted Turrets attached to the tank.
* [[Auto Gunner]]: It is a [[Gunner]] with a Mounted Turret on top of it.
* [[Auto Trapper]]: It is a [[Trapper]] with a Mounted Turret on top of it.
*[[Auto Smasher]]: It is a [[Smasher]] with a Mounted Turret on top of it. Has Health, Bullet and Movement stats which can all be upgraded 10 times each.
*[[Auto Tank]]: It is a [[Tank|Basic Tank]] with a Mounted Turret on top of it.

=== Polygons ===
*[[Defender]]: Has three evenly spaced Mounted Turrets on its body near each point. They all have free rotation.
*[[Defender (8th Anniversary)]]: Has three evenly spaced Mounted Turrets on its body near each point. They all have free rotation.
*[[Tree-Topper]]: Has five evenly spaced Mounted Turrets on its body near each point. They all have free rotation.

=== Fallen ===
*[[Fallen Overlord (8th Anniversary)]]: It is a Fallen Overlord with a Mounted Turret on top of it.

==History==
* The [[Auto Gunner]] was the first to use Mounted Turrets on August 1, 2016.
*On August 2nd, 2016, the [[Auto 5]] (at the time known as the "Auto Tank") was added.
**On the same day, the [[Auto 3]] was added (albeit unnamed) and the Auto 5 gained an extra Mounted Turret and later received its current name.
*Mounted Turrets used to default to face the front of the tank instead of the back of the tank.
**This change was likely made between the releases of the Auto Gunner and Auto Trapper.
*On August 15th, 2016, the [[Auto Trapper]] was added.
*On September 13th, 2016, the [[Auto Smasher]] was secretly added.
**This is because the developer was pushing some hotfixes and forgot he added it, as described in {{PL|https://www.reddit.com/r/Diepio/comments/52ljt5/theres_a_new_secret_class/d7lg2l4/|this Reddit post}}.
*On November 19th, 2016, it was briefly possible to control the Mounted Turrets of Auto Gunners/Trappers/Smashers by pressing H while near one. This functionality was removed after 10 minutes.
*On February 12th, 2025, the [[Auto Tank]] was added.

==Trivia ==
*[[Drones]] (when not manually controlled) exhibit very similar behavior to Mounted Turrets in the fact that they spin/follow their owner slowly when nothing is in range and attacking nearby tanks.
**The [[Battleship|Battleship's]] [[Drones#Battleship_Drones|Drones]] will not circle the player due to them being able to detect tanks and polygons from far distances.
*If a tank with a Mounted Turret on the top of its tank body is killed, then the Mounted Turret doesn't become transparent.
*The Auto Smasher’s Turret fires slightly less accurately.
