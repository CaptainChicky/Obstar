The {{PA}} is the second strongest [[Polygons|Polygon]] in [[Diep.io]], second only to the [[Alpha Pentagon]].

It grants a score of 1500 [[XP]] to the player who deals the final shot.

The Hexagon looks very similar to the [[Pentagon]] at first, leading many players to believe it is a special [[Pentagon]], however the Hexagon has a distinct cyan color, with 6 sides instead of 5.

Hexagons are significantly rarer than [[Pentagon|Pentagons]], with roughly a ratio of 50:1.

== Strategy ==

Hexagons help serve as great XP sources, giving the highest amount of all normal Polygons (excluding [[:Category:Rare Polygons|Shiny Variants]] or the [[Alpha Pentagon]]).

Due to the Hexagon being significantly stronger than most [[Polygons]], it is advised to not ram into it, as they can deal great amounts of damage, quickly leading to death.

Mathematically speaking, Hexagons are "better" than Alpha Pentagons: they have exactly half the HP and yield half the XP, but they appear more than twice as often, and most importantly, they aren't as popular.

== Trivia ==
* Before September 19, 2025, hexagons were small and had less HP and body damage. Now, they are larger and similar to arras.io Hexagons.
* Before September 19, 2025, they had 10 HP, slightly more Body Damage than a square, and gave 25 XP.
