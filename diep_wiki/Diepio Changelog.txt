'''June 7th 2016'''


-We've tweaked how penetration works:

<nowiki>*</nowiki> Bullet penetration is (and has always been) bullet health

<nowiki>*</nowiki> Before: Bullets would always deal their entire damage, even if they had little health left

<nowiki>*</nowiki> After: Bullets now deal a proportional amount of damage if they're about to die while dealing that damage

<nowiki>*</nowiki> This has some deep changes in how bullet penetration plays out in a few scenarios, ~~so let us know what you think~~

<nowiki>*</nowiki> It should make bullets behave more fairly when two players have different penetration values

<nowiki>*</nowiki> This change actually affects anything to do with damage on touch, be it tank or drones(doritos).

It also means that body damage also decreases damage taken

* Thus we have also adjusted some other values in the game to offset this change
