## Reading the references: unit and convention notes

| quantity | diep | ours | conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| tick | `mspt = 40` (`diepcustom/src/config.ts:26`) | `REF_TICK_MS = 40` | **1:1, no time conversion** |
| length | 1 gu = 50 du | 1 gu = 28 units | **du → ours: × 0.56** |
| speed | du/tick | units/ref-tick | × 0.56 |
| speed | du/tick | u/s | × 14 (`× 0.56 × 25`) |
| tank base radius | `baseSize = 50` du (`TankBody.ts:66`) | `28` | 50 du = 1 gu = 28 ✓ |
| level exponents | `1.01^(L−1)`, `1.015^(L−1)`, L 1-based | `1.01^this.level`, `this.level` 0-based | **identical** ✓ |
| barrel/bullet multipliers | `TankDefinitions.json`, 1.0 = base | `TanksConfig.js`, 1.0 = base | **1:1** ✓ |

**The `lib/tick.js` category for every new constant is called out per step.** Getting it wrong does
not fail loudly (PENDING nuance 39).

**The friction ordering difference, stated once so no step re-derives it.** diep applies
`v += A; x += v; v *= 0.9` (`diepcustom/src/Entity/Object.ts:265-275`, `applyPhysics`). We apply
`v = (v + A)·F; x += v` (`public/SHARE/Physics.js`'s `stepBody`). Both have steady state `10·A`
— ours only because `F = 10/11` was *derived* to make it so. The two differ only in **transient**:
diep's velocity error decays ×0.9/tick, ours ×0.909/tick (e-fold 0.38 s vs 0.42 s). This is why
`Physics.FRICTION = 10/11` is **not** contradicted by diep's 0.9 for tanks — but it *is* the whole
story for bullets, whose terminal speed is `thrust × F/(1−F)` and therefore 22.0× thrust at our
`BODY_FRICTION` against 10× at diep's 0.9. See Step 9.

invulnerability multiplier** — `1.0` normally, `0.0` during spawn protection, godmode, and for Arena

`common = max(minMult₁, minMult₂) × min(maxMult₁, maxMult₂)` (`Live.ts:74-75`), which is exactly the
4 / 6 / 1 shape-vs-tank-vs-projectile family we already model as `×4` baked into the base plus
`TANK_BODY_DAMAGE` and `PROJECTILE_BODY_DAMAGE`. There is no Body-Damage-points term on the receiving
side at all.

**Effect if adopted:** every source of damage to a tank becomes **2.5× stronger** at 0 Body Damage
points. Combined with our bullet damage being `4.84848 ≡ diep's 7` on a 50 HP tank, our current
time-to-kill is roughly **3.6× diep's**. So `dr` is not a small fidelity gap — it is most of why
combat here is slower than diep's.

Also unmodelled and part of the same question: diep's **mutual proration** (`Live.ts:78-84`,
README §5) — when a body dies mid-tick, both sides' damage that tick is scaled by the same surviving
fraction. We apply full per-tick damage to both sides and let the dead one die.

**RESOLVED — this is now Step 5, not a flag.** The user's call, 2026-07-31: *"damagereduction was me

### C2 — Knockback scales with the Bullet Damage stat; our column is frozen at 1 point
PENDING #16 is SHIPPED and marked do-not-re-fix: `weight` is `physics.html`'s Knockbackfactor table
× 5.25, consumed as `tick.impulse(weight / 3 × 1.6)`.

diep computes it (`Bullet.ts:88`, README §4.3.2):
```
pushFactor    = (7/3 + bulletDamagePoints) × bullet.damage × bullet.absorbtionFactor
knockback (du/tick) = receiver.absorbtionFactor × pushFactor
```
**Our column's *shape* is exactly right** — check it: Basic `(7/3+1)×1 = 3.333` → `/5` = 0.666 gu
(our `weight 3.5`); Twin `(7/3+1)×0.65 = 2.167` → 0.4333 gu (our `2.275`); Destroyer
`(7/3+1)×3×0.1 = 1.0` → 0.2 gu (our `1.05`). Every entry is `weight = 5.25 × pushFactor/5`, uniformly.

**Two findings fall out of that:**
1. **`physics.html`'s table was measured at 1 Bullet Damage point, not 0.** Our column reproduces it
   exactly at `bd = 1`. So the correct *base* (0-point) knockback is **0.7×** our current values, and
   the 7-point value is **2.8×** — a **4× span** that we do not model at all.
2. **The absolute delivery is exact at that point.** Our `weight/3×1.6` gives Basic `1.8667`
   units/ref-tick; diep at `bd = 1` gives `3.333 du/t × 0.56 = 1.8667` — **identical** ✓, and both
   integrators put the total displacement at `10 × A`. **The tank-body constant `4.48` is exact too**
   (see confirmations). So there is one finding, not two: the magnitude is right at `bd = 1`, and the
   entire missing piece is the `(7/3 + bd)/(7/3 + 1)` factor — 0.7× at 0 points, 2.8× at 7.

**If it is ever taken:** replace `tick.impulse(other.weight / 3 * 1.6)` with
`tick.impulse(other.weight * 0.112 * (7/3 + shooterBdPoints))`, which reduces to the current
expression at `bd = 1`. It needs the bullet to carry its shooter's Bullet Damage point count (it
already carries `weight`, `push`, `damage`, `pene` — one more field). **Not a planned step**: #16 is
do-not-re-fix, and this is a real balance change on top of Step 2's Bullet Damage slope.

Also unmodelled: diep's **receiver-side `absorbtionFactor`** (README §4.3.1 — Pentagon 0.5, Alpha
0.05, small Crasher 2.0, large Crasher 0.1, bosses/Mothership 0.01, Arena Closers and Maze walls 0,
drones 4). We use a `weight` mass divisor on shapes (1 / 4 / 100) — the same idea on a different axis.
This is nuance 44's "left open on purpose: the tank-vs-shape arm has the same gap"; the reference now
supplies the numbers if that is ever picked up.

### C3 — `MOVE_ACCEL_BASE` is 1.47 % high given our 0-based level
